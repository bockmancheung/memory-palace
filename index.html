Accounts & syncing

Every deck saves your progress automatically in this browser as you study. Click the account pill in the top-right corner of any page to create a free account — once you're signed in, your progress follows you to any device, and is still there the next time you log back in.

Hosting this site

This page links to each deck by filename (e.g. fichier-litteraire.html), the standard way a static website's pages link to one another. Upload all files — including the shared/ folder — to the same directory on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, etc.) and it becomes a real, shareable multi-page website.

Each deck saves its own progress independently, on this device — and to your account across devices once you sign in.
